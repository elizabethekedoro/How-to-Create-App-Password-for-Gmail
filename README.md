<h1> What is App Password?</h1>
An App Password is a unique 16-character passcode generated by your Google Account that allows apps or devices to securely access your account without using your regular password.

* App Passwords are typically used for applications or devices that do not support Google's standard sign-in process or modern authentication methods. They provide a secure way to grant access to applications without exposing your primary account password.

* Note: App Passwords are only available if 2-Step Verification (2FA) is enabled on your Google Account. If you have not enabled 2-Step Verification, you must do so before you can generate an App Password.
