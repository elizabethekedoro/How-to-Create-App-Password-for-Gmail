<h1> What is App Password?</h1>
An App Password is a unique 16-character passcode generated by your Google Account that allows apps or devices to securely access your account without using your regular password. App Passwords are typically used for applications or devices that do not support Google's standard sign-in process or modern authentication methods. They provide a secure way to grant access to applications without exposing your primary account password.
